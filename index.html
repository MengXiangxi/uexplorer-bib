About This Project

The publication list above is rendered directly from uExplorer.bib. New publications are tracked via alerts from Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, and PubMed.

Since 2025, an AI-assisted curation toolkit (blog post) paired with deterministic schema validation (scripts/bib_tool.py) is used to normalize metadata, scanner institutions, and controlled keywords.

Contributing

If you notice a missing or newly published article involving the uEXPLORER total-body PET/CT scanner, please open an issue or pull request on GitHub, or contact Xiangxi Meng.

BibTeX Entry Format

@article{author2026keyword, title={Sentence case title with <sup>18</sup>F tags}, author={Last, First M and Last2, First2}, journal={Full Journal Name}, volume={X}, number={Y}, pages={123--456}, year={2026}, doi={10.xxxx/xxxxx}, pmid={12345678}, institution={Institution 1; Institution 2}, keywords={keyword1; keyword2} }
  • Citekey: <first_author_lastname><year><keyword> (e.g., leung2021quantitative).
  • Title: Sentence case with HTML <sup> and <sub> tags for isotopes and chemical formulas.
  • Author: Complete author list in Last, First M format joined by and (no et al.).
  • Year: 4-digit issue publication year, or Just Accepted for online-first articles without volume/pages.
  • Institution & Keywords: Semicolon-separated (; ) values governed by taxonomy.json and lists.md.
